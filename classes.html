New to modeling? Key terms, explained
  • Digitals: simple photos with no makeup or filters that show agencies what you really look like.
  • Comp card: your model business card, with your best photos and measurements, sent to agencies and castings.
  • Editorial: a styled, fashion-magazine-style photo that shows your range.
  • Casting / go-see: an audition where designers and producers choose models for a show or shoot.
  • Portfolio (your “book”): your collection of best photos that you show to agencies and clients.

Parent fine print

Policies for parents & guardians

Separate classes. Kids & teens never train with adult students. Classes are grouped by age: 5–9, 10–13 and 14–17.

Parent or guardian books. Only a parent or legal guardian can book and pay for a student under 18.

Forms before the first class. A parent signs the liability waiver, parent consent form and photo/video release before the student can take part.

Parents are welcome to stay. A parent or approved adult must stay on site for ages 5–9. Parents of older students may stay or drop off.

Drop-off & pick-up. Please arrive on time. Students are released only to the parent or an adult listed on the consent form.

Communication goes through parents. Bri emails the parent, not the student, about schedules, changes and updates.

Private sessions: parent in the room. For every Kids & Teens private session, a parent or guardian must stay in the room for the entire session. No exceptions, and no closed-door coaching.

Photos & social media. Student photos and videos are shared only with a signed photo release, and never with a student’s last name or school.

Age-appropriate shows only. Show spots for minors are offered only at age-appropriate events, with a parent’s permission and a chaperone backstage.

Health & safety. Tell Bri about allergies, medical needs or injuries when you book. Students should bring water and comfortable shoes; heels are optional and age-appropriate.

Payments & refunds. Kids & teens classes are paid in full when you book. Full refund up to 7 days before the first class; after that, your payment can move to a future class.
