Behind the scenes

What’s included

  • Show concept & run of show: the creative vision and a minute-by-minute timeline
  • Scene design: themes, order and model lineups for every scene, from opener to finale
  • Custom music mix: a show soundtrack mixed scene by scene, with transitions and cues timed to the walk
  • Casting & model coordination: casting call, model selection and lineups
  • Fittings & rehearsals: walk coaching, choreography and full run-throughs
  • Designer & crew coordination: hair, makeup, stylists, stage and production team
  • Backstage management: quick changes, lineup and timing on show day
  • Show day direction: Bri calls the show from first look to final walk
  • Full production operations manual: the playbook your whole team runs the show from (see below)

Your production operations manual

  • Run of show: the minute-by-minute schedule from call time to final walk
  • Scene breakdowns: themes, looks, lineup order and designers for each scene
  • Music cue sheet: every track, transition and lighting cue, matched to the scenes
  • Model roster: contact sheet, sizes, fitting notes and assigned looks
  • Fitting & rehearsal schedule: dates, times and what each session covers
  • Crew roles & call times: who does what, and when everyone arrives
  • Backstage plan: layout map, quick-change stations and dresser assignments
  • Vendor & designer contacts: one list for hair, makeup, stylists, venue and AV
  • Show day checklist: load-in, setup, walkthrough and breakdown steps
  • Backup plans: what to do for late models, wardrobe issues, tech failures and timing changes
