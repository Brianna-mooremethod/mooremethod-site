FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need modeling experience?

No. The Intro Workshop and Runway Ready Series start from the basics. Working models use private sessions to sharpen their walk before a show or casting.

What should I wear and bring?

Everyone wears all black.

  • Heel walkers: black or clear heels, 3 inches or higher.
  • Men or male-presenting models: all black with hard-bottom shoes.
  • Kids & teens: all black with comfortable shoes. Heels are optional and age-appropriate.
  • Bring: your class shoes and water. Wear your hair pulled back and keep makeup minimal so Bri can see your posture and expressions.
Is there an age minimum?

Bri coaches ages 5 and up. Kids & Teens classes are separate from adult classes and grouped by age (5–9, 10–13, 14–17). See the parent fine print in the Kids & Teens section. Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign the consent form, and a parent must be present for private sessions.

How does the show spot work?

Runway Ready Series, Show Ready and Signature Intensive clients are placed in an upcoming show Bri coordinates as Model Coordinator. Your spot is confirmed once you finish your package and attend the show's required rehearsals and fittings.

Will I get photos?

Yes. Every adult client gets watermark-free photos from class for their portfolio, comp card and socials.

Where are classes held?

In the Atlanta area (30312). The exact address is shown when you book.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. The Runway Ready Series can be paid in two $90 payments, with the second due before Week 3.

What if I miss a class?

See the policies below. You can make up one missed group class in the next cohort.

Policies

The fine print

Deposits & refunds

The $50 deposit is non-refundable but can be moved to a future class. Full payments are refundable up to 7 days before your first class, minus the deposit. No refunds after the first class.

Rescheduling

Private sessions can be rescheduled once with 24 hours' notice. Less than 24 hours' notice or a no-show counts as a completed session.

Missed classes

You can make up one missed group class in the next cohort. Missing two or more classes may affect your show placement.

Show placement

Show spots require finishing your package and attending the show's rehearsals and fittings. Scene and look assignments are made with each show's producers and designers.

Photos & your image

Class photos may be used to promote The Moore Method, as described in the photo and model release you sign. You get a personal-use license for your class photos.

Safety & minors

Every student signs a liability waiver. Students under 18 need a signed parent or guardian consent form.
