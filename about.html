Meet your coach

Bri Moore

Bri took her first runway walk at Clark Atlanta University in 2021 and went on to walk Atlanta Fashion Week in 2024 and 2025. Along the way she moved from walking shows to coaching the models in them, then to running them — rising from Model Coordinator to Fashion Show Chair of CAU Homecoming's fashion show.

Today Bri prepares models for some of fashion's biggest stages, including New York Fashion Week, Atlanta Fashion Week, the Bronner Bros. Beauty Icon Awards and homecoming fashion shows, and has trained more than 200 models from Atlanta to Baltimore. She knows exactly what producers look for, because she's the one making the calls: who's ready, who's on time, and who gets booked again.

A Baltimore native, Clark Atlanta University graduate and founder of a youth modeling and leadership organization, Bri built The Moore Method to give every model the training — and the stage — she had to earn the hard way.

Founder · Since 2023

Be’Moore Foundation

Talent opens the door. Opportunity gets you through it. Bri built the Be’Moore Foundation to give students both: real runways to walk, mentors who invest in them, and the professional polish the industry expects.

Every program stands on three pillars:

  • Modeling. Runway training, posing and real shows, so students build confidence and experience on a professional stage.
  • Leadership. Mentorship, professional development and college and career prep, so students lead in every room they walk into.
  • Service. We don’t just model. We give back. Students serve their communities through hands-on service initiatives, because how you show up for others matters as much as how you walk.

What began in Baltimore became an official Clark Atlanta University chapter in 2024, and now reaches high school students too. In its first year alone: 5 shows and 10+ service initiatives.
